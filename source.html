The Methodology

Four steps. One complete inventory.

A continuous, update-ready workflow. Every phase builds on the last — and the cycle repeats every time you need fresh data, without rebuilding from scratch.

01

LiDAR Scanning

Jakarto-powered vehicles sweep your city at traffic speed — 2 million laser pulses per second, synchronized 72 MP 360° cameras, sub-5cm accuracy. Complete right-of-way coverage in days, not months.

02

AI Automation

Proprietary algorithms transform the point cloud into a complete baseline inventory in days. Every tree detected, located, and measured — DBH, height, crown volume, clearances. Consistent. Objective. Exhaustive.

03

Arborist Expertise

ISA Certified Arborists review every record remotely — validating species, assessing crown condition, flagging maintenance priorities, and identifying trees requiring on-site formal risk assessment.

04

TreePlotter™ Integration

Validated data flows into TreePlotter — your living urban forest command center. Work orders, inspections, reporting, planning. Re-scan. Refresh. Repeat. Forever current, never rebuilt from scratch.

Data Outputs

Every tree. Every metric.
No guesswork.

A complete, defensible digital portrait of every tree in your urban forest — attributes consistent across every block, district, and city in your program.

Tree Location
Sub-centimeter GPS-registered X/Y coordinate, street address, and parcel association for every tree.
Sub-5cm accuracy · 90% of measurements
Diameter (DBH)
Trunk diameter at breast height measured directly from the point cloud using trunk-fitting algorithms.
Typical accuracy: ±1–2 cm · R² > 0.93 vs. manual
Tree Height
Total height, height to crown base, and clearance height — all derived from direct LiDAR measurement.
Typical accuracy: ±0.3 m
Crown Dimensions
N–S and E–W spread, maximum crown spread, estimated 3D crown volume, and canopy density index.
Derived from 3D canopy point returns
Utility Clearances
Precise 3D measurement of vertical and horizontal clearances to overhead lines, cables, signs, and buildings.
Direct measurement from point cloud geometry
Planting Sites
Available vs. occupied planting locations, pit dimensions, soil or pavement type, and site constraints.
Street imagery + point cloud fusion
Species Identification
ISA Arborist-validated genus and species from bark texture, canopy form, and branching in 72 MP imagery.
85–92% genus-level accuracy; field verification for flagged records
Condition & Risk Flags
Crown condition ratings (ISA scale), visible structural concerns, maintenance priority codes, and field referral flags.
Remote ISA review · On-site referrals per TRAQ guidance
Ecosystem Services
CO&sub2; storage, annual sequestration, stormwater interception, energy savings, and canopy contribution via i-Tree in TreePlotter.
Powered by i-Tree · USDA Forest Service methodology
